Delete your FinPro account

You can permanently delete your account and all associated financial records. Deletion cannot be undone.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes your FinPro account and the user-owned records linked to it, including:

  • Profile and account information, including your email, username, and password hash.
  • Financial accounts, transactions, budgets, goals, investments, loans, insurance policies, subscriptions, cards, calendar events, and tasks.
  • AI conversations, recommendations, receipt scans, and export files.
  • Push tokens, device records, pending SMS suggestions, refresh sessions, and account audit records.

Uploaded files are removed from FinPro storage before the account record is deleted. If storage cleanup cannot complete, the account record is left intact so you can retry. Files already removed during that attempt cannot be restored.

This action is permanent. Export any records you want to keep before continuing. There is no recovery period and no way to restore a deleted account.

Delete from the Android app

  1. Open FinPro and go to Profile & Settings.
  2. Scroll to the Delete account section.
  3. Read the warning, confirm the deletion, and complete the verification step.

Accounts with a FinPro password must re-enter that password. If an account has no FinPro password, the active signed-in session and explicit confirmation are used instead.

Delete from the web

For your security, you must sign in before deleting your account from the web. After signing in, you will return to this page to complete the deletion.

Sign in to continue

If you cannot sign in, use the Android app while signed in, or contact support through the public issue tracker linked in the footer. Never post your password in a public issue.
