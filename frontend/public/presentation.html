02 — Problem & Opportunity

Navigating capital markets on gut feeling doesn't scale.

Over 100M+ Indian retail investors have entered the markets since 2020, yet lack affordable fiduciary advice, suffer from fragmented tooling, and face Western-biased algorithmic tools.

The Retail Gap

100M+ Demats. 76% Illiteracy. Zero Unified Systems.

Investors track stocks on one app, debts on another, and bank statements in inboxes. Advice comes from ungrounded generic AI or unregulated finfluencers.

Pain 1

Western Bias

Existing robo-advisors assume 401(k), US IRS brackets, and fail on Indian SIPs & MCX Gold.

Pain 2

Hallucinatory AI

Generic ChatGPT invents returns with zero knowledge of the user's actual bank records.

Pain 3

Tool Silos

Budgeting, loans, broking, and insurance exist in isolated, non-communicating applications.

FinPro Fix

One Grounded Financial OS

India-native calculations (INR, Lakhs/Crores), live NSE/BSE feeds, and Gemini 1.5 Flash bound strictly to PostgreSQL transactional truth.

04 — Key Empirical Findings

Four published findings that anchor FinPro.

1. Context RAG beats raw scale

FinGPT vs. BloombergGPT

While BloombergGPT (50B) proved domain pretraining outperforms general LLMs, FinGPT (Yang et al.) proved that RAG context injection matches domain performance at <1% the deployment cost. FinPro uses Gemini 1.5 Flash grounded in PostgreSQL.

2. Two-Stage Portfolios

R² = 0.9980

Wang et al. (2024, ESWA): Savitzky-Golay denoising + LSTM forecasting + Mean-CVaR achieved 7.54–9.38% cumulative alpha on CSI 300.

3. Novice Welfare Gains

Rossi & Utkus (JFE)

Vanguard data shows robo-advisors eliminate home-bias and cash drag, generating the highest Sharpe improvement for inexperienced retail users.

4. Indian Adoption Determinants

Banerjee (SEM N=387, 2025)

Technology Readiness & Financial Literacy are the two primary drivers of retail robo-advisory adoption in India. FinPro translates this into transparent factor explanations and educational tooltips.

08 — AI Advisory Engine

Audited context RAG vs. dangerous hallucinations.

Generic ChatGPT

The Hallucination Danger

  • ❌ Zero Account Context: Unaware whether the user has ₹5,000 or ₹5,00,000 in reserves.
  • ❌ Dangerous Advice: Recommends volatile equities to individuals struggling to service high-cost debt.
  • ❌ Fabricated Numbers: Hallucinates arbitrary return percentages and non-existent mutual funds.
  • ❌ Session Amnesia: Conversation memory evaporates across logins.
FinPro Grounded RAG

Audited Ledger Injection

  • ✔ Audited Snapshot: Prepends verified Net Worth, Liquid Cash, Monthly Burn, DTI, and 10-Factor Score.
  • ✔ Strict Guardrails: System prompt forbids mentioning numbers outside the injected JSON context.
  • ✔ Deterministic Fallback: Heuristic rule engine answers safely if Gemini quotas are exhausted.
  • ✔ PostgreSQL Memory: Conversation history persisted in chat_messages table.
