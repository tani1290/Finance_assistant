02 — Problem & Opportunity

Navigating capital markets on gut feeling doesn't scale.

Over 100M+ Indian retail investors have entered capital markets since 2020, yet lack affordable fiduciary advice, suffer from fragmented tooling, and face Western-biased algorithms.

The Retail Gap

100M+ Demats. 76% Illiteracy. Zero Unified Systems.

Investors track stocks on one app, debts on another, and bank statements in inboxes. Advice comes from ungrounded generic AI or unregulated finfluencers.

Pain 1

Western Bias

Existing robo-advisors assume 401(k), US IRS brackets, and fail on Indian SIPs, PPF & MCX Gold.

Pain 2

Hallucinatory AI

Generic ChatGPT invents returns with zero knowledge of the user's actual bank records or DTI.

Pain 3

Tool Silos

Budgeting, loans, broking, and insurance exist in isolated, non-communicating applications.

FinPro Fix

One Grounded Financial OS

India-native calculations (INR, Lakhs/Crores), live NSE/BSE feeds, and Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite bound strictly to PostgreSQL transactional truth.

04 — Key Empirical Findings

Four published findings that anchor FinPro.

1. Context RAG beats raw scale

FinGPT vs. BloombergGPT

While BloombergGPT (50B) proved domain pretraining outperforms general LLMs, FinGPT (Yang et al.) proved that RAG context injection matches domain performance at <1% the deployment cost. FinPro leverages Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite grounded strictly in PostgreSQL records.

2. Novice Welfare Gains

Rossi & Utkus (JFE)

Vanguard data shows robo-advisors eliminate home-bias and cash drag, generating the highest Sharpe improvement for inexperienced retail users.

3. Indian Adoption Determinants

Banerjee (SEM N=387, 2025)

Technology Readiness & Financial Literacy are the two primary drivers of retail robo-advisory adoption in India. FinPro translates this into transparent factor explanations and educational tooltips.

10 — AI Advisory Engine

Audited context RAG vs. dangerous hallucinations.

Generic ChatGPT

The Hallucination Danger

  • ❌ Zero Account Context: Unaware whether the user has ₹5,000 or ₹5,00,000 in liquid reserves.
  • ❌ Dangerous Advice: Recommends volatile equities to individuals struggling to service high-cost debt.
  • ❌ Fabricated Numbers: Hallucinates arbitrary return percentages and non-existent mutual funds.
  • ❌ Session Amnesia: Conversation memory evaporates across logins.
FinPro Grounded RAG

Audited Ledger Injection (Gemini 3.5)

  • ✔ Audited Snapshot: Prepends verified Net Worth, Liquid Cash, Monthly Burn, DTI, and 10-Factor Score.
  • ✔ Strict Guardrails: Native systemInstruction forbids fabricating numbers outside injected JSON context.
  • ✔ Watchdog & Fallback: 12-second timeout + heuristic rule engine answers safely if quota is exhausted.
  • ✔ PostgreSQL Memory: Multi-tenant conversation history persisted in chat_messages table.
